# My Favorite Series: Lucifer 

**Genre**: Crime, Fantasy, Drama  
**Main Character**: Lucifer Morningstar (played by Tom Ellis)  
**Why I love it**:  
- Witty dialogue and clever humor  
- Deep character development  
- Unique blend of crime-solving and celestial drama  
- Strong themes of redemption and identity

##  Favorite Quote
> "People don't arrive broken. They start with passion and yearning until something comes along that disabuses them of those notions."
